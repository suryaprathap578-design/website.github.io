From design to erection

Four stages of steel.

Each stage produces a defined deliverable and is checked before the next begins.

ST—01

Structural design

Frame analysis, member sizing, bracing and connection design.

ST—02

Detailing

General arrangement drawings, connection details and fabrication drawings.

ST—03

Fabrication

Cutting, drilling, welding and surface protection, checked against drawings.

ST—04

Erection

Sequenced erection, alignment, bolting and temporary stability on site.
