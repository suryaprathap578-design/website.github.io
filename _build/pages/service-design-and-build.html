Design & build.

One team. One contract. One point of accountability — from the first drawing to the day you receive the keys.

Why design & build

Fewer handovers. Fewer surprises.

In a conventional project, design, engineering and construction are separate contracts. Gaps between them become delays, variations and disputes. Design & build closes those gaps.

Conventional route

Separate designers and builders

  • Multiple contracts to manage
  • Design issued before buildability is tested
  • Coordination issues found on site
  • Responsibility split between parties
SUBHA INFRA design & build

One integrated team

  • A single contract and point of contact
  • Design developed with construction in mind
  • Coordination resolved on drawings, not on site
  • Clear accountability for the outcome
Scope

Everything from planning to handover.
