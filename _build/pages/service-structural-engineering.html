Overview

Structure that serves the architecture.

We engineer RCC buildings, steel structures and pre-engineered buildings — from analysis and member design to foundations and fully detailed drawings.

Engaged early, structural engineering protects both the design and the budget: grids are rationalised, spans and cantilevers are tested while they can still change, and foundations are designed to real soil data rather than assumptions.

A complete structural package.

Clear drawings and documentation your contractor can build from — or that we execute ourselves.
