Who we are

Building beyond conventional.

SUBHA INFRA is an integrated architecture, engineering and construction company. We bring design, structure and execution together under one team, so every building is conceived, engineered and delivered as a single, coherent idea.

Most projects lose quality at the handover between disciplines. We remove that gap. Architects, structural engineers and site teams work from the same drawings, the same priorities and the same standard of finish — from the first concept sketch to the final handover.

  • 01Design thinking

    Spaces shaped by purpose, light, context and the way people will use them.

  • 02Structural intelligence

    Efficient, safe systems resolved alongside the architecture — not after it.

  • 03Material innovation

    Steel, glass, cladding and finishes specified for performance and longevity.

  • 04Disciplined execution

    Coordinated construction that delivers exactly what was designed.

Engineering expertise

Where design meets structural intelligence.

Behind every elevation is a structural system. We analyse, size and detail it with the same care we give the architecture.

● Steel Structures · Construction stage
E—01

Structural analysis

Load paths, gravity, wind and seismic actions modelled to size every member with confidence.

E—02

RCC structures

Columns, beams, slabs and cores designed and detailed for residential and commercial buildings.

E—03

Structural steel systems

Frames, trusses, mezzanines and heavy steel designed for efficiency and fast erection.

E—04

PEB engineering

Tapered primary frames, purlins, girts and bracing optimised for clear spans and tonnage.

E—05

Foundation design

Isolated, combined and raft footings designed to soil investigation data and loads.

E—06

Steel fabrication

Fabrication drawings, connection detailing and shop coordination for accurate site fit-up.

E—07

Roofing & cladding

Roof sheeting, wall cladding, insulation, skylights and drainage specified as one envelope.

E—08

Facade systems

ACP, aluminium, glazing and metal facades with engineered brackets and sub-frames.

Technical detailing
