Service 01

Architectural design.

Buildings shaped by purpose, proportion and place — developed from first concept to construction-ready drawings.

Overview

Good architecture is specific: to its site, its climate, its budget and the people who will live or work in it.

We design independent houses, villas, multi-storey residences, commercial buildings and workplaces — always with the structural engineer at the table, so design ambition and buildability grow together.

Design stages

From idea to issue-for-construction.

Each stage ends with a clear review, so decisions are made at the right time — and changes cost least.

Stage A

Concept

Site study, brief analysis, zoning, massing options and design direction.

Stage B

Schematic design

Developed plans, elevations and sections, with structural grid coordination.

Stage C

Visualisation

3D exterior views to review form, material and light before construction.

Stage D

Working drawings

Dimensioned, detailed drawings for approvals, tendering and construction.

  • Offices and corporate buildings
