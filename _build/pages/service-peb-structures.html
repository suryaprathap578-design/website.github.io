What is a PEB?

Steel, placed exactly where it works.

A pre-engineered building uses tapered, built-up steel frames whose depth follows the bending forces along their length. Steel goes where it is needed — and nowhere else.

Frames, purlins, girts and cladding are designed as a system, fabricated off site and bolted together on site. The result is a large-span building that is economical in steel and quick to erect.

PEB vs conventional steel

Choosing the right system.

Both have their place. We recommend the system that suits your spans, loads, programme and budget.

ConsiderationPEBConventional structural steel
Frame membersTapered built-up sections sized to forcesStandard hot-rolled sections
Best suited toLarge clear-span, regular buildingsComplex geometry, heavy or irregular loads
Steel efficiencyTypically lighter for long spansCan be heavier for equivalent spans
ErectionBolted system, fast on siteMore site work for complex connections
