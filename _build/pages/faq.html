Packages & pricing

Are the package rates fixed prices?

The rates on the packages page are indicative per-sq-ft rates for standard specifications. Final costs depend on site conditions, size, structure, specifications and scope, so we confirm the price in a written proposal based on your actual project.

Are the package inclusions fixed?

Each package lists what is included as standard, what is optional and what is not part of it. The final scope is confirmed in your proposal and can be tailored.

What is not usually included?

Typically statutory fees, utility connection charges and specialist investigations such as soil testing, unless agreed in the proposal. Each package page lists its exclusions.

Steel, PEB & structures

What is the difference between PEB and conventional steel?

PEB uses tapered built-up members designed as a complete system, and suits large, regular clear-span buildings. Conventional steel uses standard rolled sections and handles irregular geometry and heavy loads well.

Do you need a soil investigation report?

For foundation design, yes — we strongly recommend a soil investigation by a qualified agency. We can advise on the scope of investigation needed.

Do you handle fabrication and erection?

Our steel, PEB, facade and tensile packages include fabrication and erection coordination. Whether supply, fabrication and erection are within our contract is confirmed in each proposal.
