Overview

Form found by forces.

A tensile fabric structure gets its strength from shape and pre-tension. Getting that shape right — together with masts, cables, anchors and drainage — is an engineering exercise as much as a design one.

We form-find the membrane, pattern the fabric, design the steel support system and foundations for wind uplift, and coordinate fabrication, tensioning and installation on site.
