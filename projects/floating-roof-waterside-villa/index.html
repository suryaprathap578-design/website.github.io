Overview

This residential design study explores how plan, structure and facade are resolved together. It represents a typology SUBHA INFRA designs and engineers for independent houses, villas and multi-storey homes.

Scope of services

  • Architectural concept and space planning
  • Elevations, sections and working drawings
  • RCC structural design and detailing
  • Facade and material specification
  • Construction execution under Design & Build

Architectural concept

A two-storey villa with deep floating roof planes, full-height glazing and a reflecting water channel along the garden edge.

Structural system

Typically an RCC framed structure of columns, beams and slabs on footings designed to site soil data. Cantilevered balconies and deep slab edges are sized and detailed in the structural design so the architecture can be built exactly as drawn.

Materials & specifications

  • Timber-finish soffits
  • Rendered piers
  • Full-height glazing
  • Timber louvres
  • Water channel
