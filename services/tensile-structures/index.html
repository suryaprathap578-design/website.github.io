Tensile fabric structures.

Light, expressive membrane structures for shade, shelter and arrival — form-found, engineered for wind and weather, and built to last.

● Conical membrane canopies
Overview

Form found by forces.

A tensile fabric structure gets its strength from shape and pre-tension. Getting that shape right — together with masts, cables, anchors and drainage — is an engineering exercise as much as a design one.

We form-find the membrane, pattern the fabric, design the steel support system and foundations for wind uplift, and coordinate fabrication, tensioning and installation on site.

Applications

Where we use them.

PVC-coated polyester, PTFE and HDPE shade fabrics — selected for span, translucency, life and budget.

01

Car parking shade structures

Cantilever and double-cantilever membrane car parks for offices, factories, apartments and malls.

02

EV charging canopies

Weather-protected charging bays with integrated lighting and cable routing provisions.

03

Entrance canopies

Signature arrival structures for homes, offices, hotels, showrooms and campuses.

04

Conical & umbrella canopies

Mast-supported cones for plazas, food courts, amphitheatres and outdoor seating.

05

Shade sails

Hypar sails for courtyards, terraces, play areas, pool decks and landscapes.

06

Walkway & drop-off covers

Covered walkways, bus bays and loading-dock membranes.

Our scope
  1. Concept & 3D form-finding
  2. Membrane patterning & fabric selection
  3. Structural design of masts, frames & cables
  4. Foundation design for wind uplift
  5. Fabrication & anti-corrosive finishes
  6. Tensioning, installation & rainwater drainage
