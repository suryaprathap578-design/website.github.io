Our process

Ten stages. One continuous thread.

A defined path from your first call to the day you take over the building — with a clear deliverable, and a clear decision, at every stage.

01
Stage 01

Client consultation

We listen first: your goals, site, budget, timeline and the way the building will be used.

  • Initial meeting or call
  • Site and document review
  • Agreement on next steps
02
Stage 02

Requirement analysis

The brief is translated into measurable requirements — areas, spans, loads, services and constraints.

  • Area and space programme
  • Site constraints and regulations
  • Soil data needs identified
03
Stage 03

Concept development

Massing, zoning and design direction explored and tested against the brief and budget.

  • Concept options
  • Preliminary structural strategy
  • Client review
04
Stage 04

Architectural planning

The approved concept is developed into plans, elevations, sections and 3D visualisation.

  • Floor plans and elevations
  • 3D visualisation
  • Working drawings
05
Stage 05

Structural engineering

Analysis, member and foundation design, and fully detailed structural drawings.

  • Analysis and design
  • Foundation design
  • Structural drawings
06
Stage 06

Technical coordination

Architecture, structure, facade and services aligned on drawings — so clashes are solved before site.

  • Cross-discipline review
  • Specification finalisation
  • Issue for construction
07
Stage 07

Execution planning

Sequencing, procurement, fabrication schedules and site logistics planned in detail.

  • Construction schedule
  • Procurement and fabrication plan
  • Site safety planning
08
Stage 08

Construction & installation

Civil, structural, steel, facade and finishing works executed and supervised on site.

  • Site execution
  • Steel erection and installation
  • Progress reporting
● Construction stage
09
Stage 09

Quality review

Stage-wise inspection against approved drawings and specifications, with issues closed out.

  • Fabrication and installation checks
  • Finishing inspection
  • Snag list and close-out
10
Stage 10

Project handover

Final walkthrough, documentation and handover of a building ready for use.

  • Final inspection
  • Drawings and documentation
  • Handover
